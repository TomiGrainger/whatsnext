One row per person: contact details, how many events they've been to, hands raised, questions asked. The mailing-list export.

One row per person per event — who was in which room, what they do, how they arrived, and how much they took part.

Every answer anyone has ever given, with the topic and question it belonged to. The full picture, and the biggest file.

These are plain CSV — they open in Excel or Numbers, and import into HubSpot, Mailchimp or anything else if you ever move. Unsubscribed people are marked rather than removed, so you can filter them out and still know they asked.
