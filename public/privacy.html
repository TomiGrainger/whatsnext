PRIVACY

What we dowith your details

Short version: your answers are anonymous, your name and email are only used to send you what you asked for, and you can have them deleted at any time by tapping one link.

What we collect

When you check in

Your first name, what you do (chosen from a list), and how you're arriving (an emoji). Your name stays on your own phone. It is used to show the room how it's made up tonight, and it is never attached to anything you answer.

While the event runs

Everything you tap — how you voted, words you added, questions you asked, challenges you posted. These are kept as anonymous room data. They carry what you do and how you arrived, so the results can be broken down ("founders said this, students said that"), and nothing that says who you are.

Each phone is given a random token by your own browser. In our records that token is replaced by a one-way hash that is different at every event, so your answers group together within one evening and cannot be connected to you, or to you at any other event. We never store the token itself.

If you ask for the results

Your email address and name, so we can send you the debrief and let you know about what we're running next. Same if you tap an offer or a donation button.

What we don't collect

No IP addresses — the server keeps no access log at all. No cookies for guests. No location, no device fingerprinting, no advertising trackers, no analytics. The only third party any page contacts is Google Fonts, which will see your IP address in order to serve the typeface.

The consequence worth understanding

Once an event is over, nobody — including us — can find out what any named person said. There is no path in our records from an email address to an answer. That is deliberate, and it cuts both ways: if you ask us to delete your details, we remove your name, your email and the record of which events you came to, but your answers are not affected, because there is nothing personal left in them to remove.

Where it's kept

The app and its data are hosted on Fly.io on servers in London, United Kingdom. That means your personal information is stored overseas. Emails are sent through Google Workspace.

Australian Privacy Principle 8 requires us to tell you this before you hand anything over, which is why it's here rather than in the small print.

How long we keep it

Contact details are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until they've clearly gone stale. Anonymous event data is kept indefinitely, because it is no longer about anybody.

Getting your details out

Every email we send carries a one-tap unsubscribe and a delete-my-details link. Deleting removes your name, your address and your attendance record from everything we hold, and stops us contacting you again.

During an event you can also open your profile on your phone and tap Delete my details, which clears your check-in, your profile, and any questions or challenges you posted from that room.

You can also just email us and ask. You have the right to ask what we hold about you and to have mistakes corrected.

Email

We only email people who gave us their address at an event. Every message identifies who it's from and carries a working unsubscribe link, as the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) requires. Unsubscribing takes effect immediately.

Complaints

If something here doesn't sit right, tell us first — we'd rather fix it. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
